Work

Field campaigns, grants, papers, data, and talks. Theme essays are on Projects. Bios are on People.

Field

Temperate rainforest geoecology. Yellow-cedar decline plots on west Chichagof Island, Tongass National Forest (Sheet’ká Kwáan territory). 2026 eddy-covariance at S Douglas fen near Juneau (CSAT+7200; USFS). Jessica Murray and Aaron MacDonald.

Native and urban Chicagoland. Nachusa Grasslands (Dixon, IL); UIC James Woodworth Prairie (JWP); Chicago CROCUS urban soils; Urban Growers Collective / PRL urban-agriculture GHG plots; Green Era Campus.

AI for natural methane. FLUXNET-CH4 / UpCH4 synthesis; ATMOS / AmeriFlux site US-AMS; ESIIL AI for Natural Methane working group.
